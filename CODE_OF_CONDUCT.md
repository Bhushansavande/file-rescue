# Code of Conduct

We want File Rescue to be a friendly, respectful project for everyone, whatever their experience level, background or location.

## Our standards

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners and with people who have just lost data.
- Respect different opinions and ways of working; give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully and help others improve.
- Keep discussions about the project; no harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Never post other people's private data or recovered personal files.

## Responsibilities

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and contributions that do not follow this code, and may block people who repeatedly break it.

## Reporting

Report problems privately through GitHub's **Security → Report a vulnerability** feature or by contacting the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled respectfully and confidentially.

This code applies in all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests and discussions.
